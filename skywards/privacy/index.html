Skywards — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

The short version

Skywards collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. Your flights are stored on your device and are not sent to me. The app does go online to look up flight details, weather and maps; what is sent in those requests is described below.

Your data stays on your device

Your flights, logbook, reminders, loyalty programme numbers, name, profile photo and settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework and app storage, and shared only with the Skywards widget on the same device. They are not synced to any server of mine, not shared with any third party, and not accessible to me. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Flight lookups

When you add or refresh a flight, Skywards sends the flight number (and, when you search by city, the two airport codes) to public flight-data sources to fetch the route, times, terminals and aircraft type. These include adsbdb, the OpenSky Network, adsb.lol and publicly available flight-status pages. The requests contain no name, account or device identifier from the app, but as with any internet request, the service receiving it can see your IP address. Each service's own privacy policy applies to what it does with that.

If you choose to enter your own AirLabs API key in Settings, flight lookups are also sent to AirLabs using that key. The key is stored only on your device.

Weather, maps and images

To show weather for a flight, Skywards sends the airport's location and the date to Open-Meteo; aviation weather reports (METAR) come from aviationweather.gov by airport code. Maps and the globe are drawn by Apple Maps. Aircraft photos and airline logos are downloaded from public image sources (planespotters.net, Wikipedia and pics.avs.io) by aircraft type or airline code. None of these requests include who you are or your flight history.

Camera, calendar and photos

Camera: used only when you choose to scan a boarding pass. The text is read on your device; the image is not uploaded.
Calendar: if you tap "Add to Calendar", Skywards writes that flight to your calendar. It asks for write-only access and cannot read your existing events.
Photos: used to save a replay video or stats card you export, and to pick a profile photo if you want one. The profile photo stays on your device.

Notifications

Pre-flight reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server is involved.

Sharing

Boarding-pass cards, stats cards, videos and CSV exports leave your device only when you share or save them yourself through the standard iOS share sheet.

Children's privacy

Skywards does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Third-party services

Skywards does not integrate any advertising networks, analytics platforms, or tracking SDKs of any kind. The only third parties involved are the data sources listed above, contacted directly from your device when a lookup is needed.

Not an official travel record

Flight details shown in Skywards come from public sources and may be out of date or wrong. Always confirm times, gates and terminals with your airline.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date above.
