The app that started as a sticky note.

I'm a chronic forgetter — not the dramatic kind, the quiet daily kind where you remember to pay the bill on the way to the kitchen and completely forget by the time you sit down. Tasks in Reminders, birthdays in Contacts, everything else on a sticky note stuck to the monitor.

I built ScatteredBrain because I wanted one place — not four apps — for the ordinary stuff life throws at you. It's not a productivity system. There's no "second brain" philosophy here. Just a quiet helper that keeps score so you don't have to.

No account. No subscription. Everything on your phone, yours completely.
