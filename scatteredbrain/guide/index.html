ScatteredBrain · User Guide

Your quiet helper for the stuff life makes you forget

ScatteredBrain is a daily organiser for people who forget things. Tasks, birthdays, bills, notes, and activity logs — all in one place, all on your phone. No account. No cloud. No subscription.

iOS 17+ iPhone & iPad 100% on-device Free forever
🔒
Your data stays on your phone

Nothing leaves your device. No analytics, no third-party trackers, no hidden syncing. If you delete the app, your data goes with it — which is why the backup feature matters.

Adding tasks

Tap the purple + button (bottom right of the Home tab) to open QuickCapture — a fast input sheet designed to get something out of your head in under 10 seconds.

QuickCapture

  1. 1
    Type what's on your mind
    Just dump it — "call dentist", "buy birthday gift", "pay electricity bill". Don't worry about formatting.
  2. 2
    Pick a time chip
    Tap Now, 1h, Tonight, or Tomorrow — or tap the schedule card to pick a custom date and time.
  3. 3
    Set a priority (optional)
    Low / Medium / High chips appear below the time row. Tap one if it matters — otherwise leave it as Low.
  4. 4
    Tap Capture
    The task is saved immediately and appears in the right time slot on your dashboard.
🤖
AI button in QuickCapture

Tap the AI button (top right of the QuickCapture sheet) to switch to natural-language input. Type "pay rent tomorrow morning urgent" and the app extracts the task, date, and priority automatically. See the AI Quick-Add section for details.

Completing tasks

Tap the circle on the right side of any task card, or swipe right, to mark it done. What happens next is designed to feel like a small celebration.

The drum roll

Every time you complete a task, the phone plays a short haptic drum roll — light taps building to a single heavy impact — followed by a chime. It's a small ritual that makes completion feel earned rather than administrative.

Log as a win?

After completing any task, a toast nudge appears at the bottom of the screen for four seconds:

Log it

Wins are for the completions that feel significant — not every task needs to be logged. The nudge auto-dismisses if you ignore it.

Recurring tasks

Any task can be set to repeat. Tap More options when adding a task and choose a frequency.

FrequencyHow it works
DailyEvery day, or weekdays only. Pre-generates 30 days ahead and extends automatically.
WeeklyPick specific days (Mon/Wed/Fri…). Pre-generates 8 weeks ahead and extends.
MonthlyChoose Start Date (same date each month), Day of Month, or Weekday Pattern (e.g. first Monday). 12 months generated ahead.
QuarterlyEvery 3 months. 4 instances generated ahead.
AnnuallyOnce a year. 3 years generated ahead.
HourlyRepeats every hour between a start and end time. Useful for medication, hydration, or breaks.

Deleting a recurring task

When you swipe to delete a recurring task, the app asks:

🗑 Delete this event only 🗑 Delete entire series

"This event only" removes just the one occurrence. "Entire series" removes all future instances.

💡
Monthly repeat — "Start From" date

When you choose Day of Month or Weekday Pattern, the date picker label changes to Start From. This sets the month the series begins — the actual first occurrence is calculated from the pattern.

Bills & Renewals

ScatteredBrain includes a built-in recurring activity scheduler for the bills and renewals that come around every year without fail — electricity, internet, car insurance, income tax. Instead of creating these as recurring tasks manually, the app offers to schedule them for you.

The annual scheduling prompt

When you open the app and there are bills or renewals not yet scheduled for the current year, a sheet appears automatically. It shows every enabled activity with how many tasks it would add and when.

  1. 1
    Select what to schedule
    All enabled activities are pre-checked. Deselect anything you handle differently or don't want on your task list.
  2. 2
    Tap "Add to [year]" or "Schedule remaining months"
    If it's already mid-year, the button reads "Schedule remaining [Month]–Dec" so only the months still ahead are created — not the ones already past.
  3. 3
    Tasks appear on your dashboard
    Each bill or renewal is added as a regular task on its due date, where it can be completed, snoozed, or rescheduled like any other task.

AI prompts for bills

In the AI Quick-Add view (tap the AI button in QuickCapture), typing keywords like "pay", "bill", "insurance", "renewal", or "service my car" shows a row of chips for common recurring activities. Tap one to pre-fill the task input with the right title and category.

💡
Enabling and disabling activities

The scheduling prompt only shows activities you've enabled. To customise the list — disable activities you don't need, change due dates, or add your own — go to Settings → Recurring Activities.

AI Quick-Add

Type naturally and let the app figure out the task, date, and priority. No special syntax — just say it like you'd say it to a friend.

How to access it

Tap the purple + FAB → QuickCapture opens → tap the AI button in the top right corner of the sheet. The view switches to a text-first input with suggested prompt chips.

  1. 1
    Type what you need to do
    "Pay rent tomorrow morning urgent", "book dentist next Tuesday", "call mum this evening".
  2. 2
    The app reads it
    Dates, times, priorities, and task names are extracted on-device. A preview shows what was detected before anything is saved.
  3. 3
    Review and confirm
    Adjust anything in the preview card, then tap Save. The AI is a first draft, not a final answer.

On-device vs. cloud AI

ModePrivacySetup
On-device (default) Nothing leaves your phone Works immediately, no API key needed
Cloud (Gemini / OpenAI / Claude) Text is sent to the provider's servers Paste your own API key in Settings → AI Engine

API keys are stored securely

Your API key is stored in the iOS Keychain — not in a file that gets backed up or synced. You'll need to re-enter it if you reinstall the app.

People tab

The People tab is a relationship manager — not just a birthday calendar. It tracks everyone's special dates and also surfaces any tasks you've added that relate to that person.

Adding a person

  1. 1
    Tap + in the top right
    The app shows contact suggestions from your phone's Contacts app. Tap a chip to pre-fill the name and phone number, or enter manually.
  2. 2
    Add special dates
    Tap Add Date to add a birthday, anniversary, engagement, pet birthday, or custom occasion. Multiple dates per person are supported.
  3. 3
    Add relationships (optional)
    Link the person to a relative — e.g. a "Spouse" relationship with their own birthday. One contact card can track an entire family.

Activity Log

The Activity tab is a quick daily record for recurring physical and personal habits — without needing a separate tracker app. Log exercise, sleep, water intake, and custom activities in a few taps.

Logging an entry

  1. 1
    Tap the Activity tab
    Or tap Track it on the habit nudge toast that appears after completing a recurring task — it takes you directly here.
  2. 2
    Pick a category
    Built-in types: 🏋️ Gym, 🏃 Run, 🚶 Walk, 🏊 Swim, 🚴 Cycle, ⚽ Sport, 🧘 Yoga, 😴 Sleep, 💧 Water, 🧘‍♂️ Meditation, and more — or create a custom one.
  3. 3
    Add quantity and a note (optional)
    "30 min run, felt sluggish" — then tap Log. The entry is added with today's date and time.

The Activity view shows your recent entries and streak counts per category so you can see patterns over time at a glance.

💡
Habit nudge after recurring tasks

Complete a recurring task (weekly gym session, daily walk reminder…) and a 💪 Log to Activity? toast appears automatically. Tap Track it to jump straight here without navigating manually.

Backup & Restore

Since everything lives on your phone, a backup is your only safety net. ScatteredBrain does this automatically and also lets you do it manually.

Automatic backups

The app saves a rolling backup every 6 hours whenever you use it. It keeps the 5 most recent backups.

Files app → On My iPhone → ScatteredBrain → Backups → ScatteredBrain-YYYY-MM-DD-HHmm.json
⚠️
After an app update

iOS updates can rarely wipe app data during a SwiftData migration. Your most recent automatic backup is the fastest way to recover — open Files app, find the latest .json in the Backups folder, then follow the restore steps below.

Creating a manual backup

  1. 1
    Open Settings (⋯ More → Settings)
    From the Home tab, tap the ⋯ icon in the top right, then Settings.
  2. 2
    Scroll to Backup → Create Backup
    The app generates a ScatteredBrain-Backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json file.
  3. 3
    Tap Share Backup File
    Save to iCloud Drive, email it to yourself, or AirDrop to your Mac — anywhere outside the phone.

Restoring from a backup

  1. 1
    Settings → Restore from Backup
    Opens the iOS file picker.
  2. 2
    Navigate to your backup file
    For an auto-backup: Browse → On My iPhone → ScatteredBrain → Backups. For a manual one, wherever you saved it.
  3. 3
    Tap the file
    The app shows a summary of what was found. Import is additive — it never overwrites data already on the device.

Tips & tricks

✨
QuickCapture is for brain-dumping

Don't overthink QuickCapture. Type it fast, pick a rough time, hit Capture. You can always tap the card later to refine it — the goal is to get it out of your head, not to fill in every field right now.

✨
Use categories for bulk actions

Long-press a category chip in the Home tab to bulk-complete or bulk-ignore all tasks in that category at once — useful for clearing out a finished project in one move.

✨
Contact detail view finds related tasks automatically

Tap any person in the People tab. The detail view scans your task list and shows every task that mentions their name — no manual tagging needed. Good for seeing everything you're doing for or with that person in one place.

✨
Schedule bills for the rest of the year, not from January

If you open the Bills & Renewals prompt in, say, August, it shows "Schedule remaining Aug–Dec" — so only tasks from now through December are created. No duplicate tasks for months already past.

✨
Search finds everything

Tap the 🔍 icon to search across all tasks — including completed and skipped ones. Filter by category, persona, or status. Great for finding what you did last month.

✨
Protect critical tasks from Auto-Ignore

The app quietly hides tasks that have been overdue for more than 15 days. For things you genuinely must not miss, turn on Protect from Auto-Ignore in More Options when creating the task.

✨
Warranty tracker pays for itself

Add your appliances, electronics, and furniture with their warranty end dates. The app reminds you a month before expiry — so you actually get to use the warranty.

✨
Waiting On keeps other people accountable

Every time you're waiting for a reply, a delivery, or an approval — log it in Waiting On. When it arrives, mark it received. No more "did I follow up on that?" anxiety.
