It started as a weekend with my daughter

One weekend, my 9-year-old daughter and I wanted to play charades. We tried a few apps on the App Store — and one by one they asked us to pay: to unlock more word decks, to remove ads, to keep playing past a point.

A game meant to bring us closer kept stopping to ask for money.

So I decided to build the one I wished existed — free for every parent and child, with no strings attached.

That became Forehead Flip: a charades game that's completely free, with the ability to create your own word decks using AI themes — also free. Ozora followed, built on the same belief. If you enjoy them, that's the whole point.
