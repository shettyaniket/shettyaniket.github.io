Ozora — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 July 2026

What we collect

Ozora does not collect any personal data. The app does not require an account, does not track you, and does not send your information to me or to any third party. The festival timetable works offline.

Favourites & reminders

The artists you favourite and your personal schedule are stored only on your device. Set reminders are delivered through local notifications scheduled on your device — no information is sent to a server to make them work.

Third-party services

Ozora does not integrate any advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Children's privacy

Ozora does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
