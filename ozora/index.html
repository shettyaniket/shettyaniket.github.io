Ozora

Your pocket companion to the Ozora Festival. An offline timetable for every stage, a personal lineup of your favourite artists, and reminders so you never miss a set — all ad-free and completely free.

Download on the App Store Privacy

Everything you need on the dancefloor

From the Main Stage to The Dome, Pumpui, Dragon Nest, Ambyss and beyond.

📶 Offline timetable

The complete, up-to-date schedule for every stage — working entirely offline. No signal in Dádpuszta? No problem.

⭐ Personalized lineup

Favourite the artists you want to see and they automatically build your own personal schedule.

🔔 Set reminders

Get a notification before your favourite artists take the stage, so you never miss a set.

🎛️ Stage-by-stage filtering

Sort the timetable by day or by stage to instantly see what's happening where.

🔒 100% privacy focused

Zero third-party tracking, no personal data collected, and absolutely no ads. Your data is your own.

🎉 Completely free

No in-app purchases, no subscriptions. A passion project to enhance the festival for everyone.

Built for the community

By festival-goers, for festival-goers.

Ozora is a labour of love, completely independent and not affiliated with the official Ozora Festival organization. The goal is simple: to help you seamlessly navigate the music, art, and culture of Ozora so you can focus on the dancefloor.

Schedule data is subject to change based on the final festival releases. See you in paradise! ✨
