What it means

I fall at your feet and bow, I drink in your form with my eyes, embrace you with love and wave the lamp — so says Nama. You alone are mother and father, kin and friend, learning and wealth; you are my all, O god of gods. Whatever I do, by body, speech, mind or nature, I offer it all to Narayana.
