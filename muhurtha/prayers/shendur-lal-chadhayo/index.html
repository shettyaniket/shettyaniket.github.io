Shendur Lal Chadhayo

An aarti to Ganpati, sung in Maharashtra and the north at Ganesh Chaturthi.

Verse (Devanagari)

शेंदूर लाल चढ़ायो अच्छा गजमुख को दोंदिल लाल बिराजे सुत गौरीहर को हाथ लिये गुड़ लड्डू साईं सुरवर को महिमा कहे न जाय लागत हूँ पद को जय जय श्री गणराज विद्यासुखदाता धन्य तुम्हारा दर्शन मेरा मन रमता अष्टौ सिद्धि दासी संकट को बैरी विघ्नविनाशन मंगल मूरत अधिकारी कोटिसु रोंमें बेटा जात न छोड़ी संकट से उबारो सेवक जन झुकारी

Transliteration

Shendur lal chadhayo achchha gajamukh ko dondil lal biraje sut Gaurihar ko hath liye gud laddu sai survar ko mahima kahe na jaay lagat hun pad ko Jai jai shri Ganaraj vidyasukhadata dhanya tumara darshan mera man ramata Ashtau siddhi dasi sankat ko bairi vighna vinashan mangal murat adhikari kotisu ron me beta jaat na chhodi sankat se ubaro sevak jan jhukari

What it means

Red vermilion adorns the fair elephant-faced one; the round-bellied lord, son of Gauri and Har, shines bright, a sweet laddu in his hand. Victory to Ganaraj, giver of learning and ease — blessed is your sight, and my heart delights in it. The eight powers are his handmaids and trouble his foe; he is the auspicious one who destroys every obstacle.
