Brahmarpanam

Said before a meal, from the Bhagavad Gita (4.24).

Mantrathe Gita

Quick facts

Type
Mantra
Deity
the Gita
When it is sung
Said before a meal, from the Bhagavad Gita (4.24).
Languages
Roman and Devanagari here; ten scripts in the app

Verse (Devanagari)

ब्रह्मार्पणं ब्रह्म हविर्ब्रह्माग्नौ ब्रह्मणा हुतम् ब्रह्मैव तेन गन्तव्यं ब्रह्मकर्मसमाधिना

Transliteration

Brahmarpanam brahma havir brahmagnau brahmana hutam brahmaiva tena gantavyam brahma-karma-samadhina

What it means

The offering is Brahman, the oblation is Brahman, poured by Brahman into the fire of Brahman; one who sees Brahman in every act reaches Brahman.

Read it in your language

In the Muhurtha app you can read Brahmarpanam in Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Tamil and Telugu, count it on a japa mala, and open a link to hear it sung — all offline, with no account and no ads.
