Prayers, aartis & mantras

Every aarti, chalisa, mantra and stotra in Muhurtha, with its full text and what it means.

These are traditional verses, long out of copyright. Each page has the words in Devanagari and in Roman transliteration, with a plain-English meaning. In the app you can also read every one in Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Tamil or Telugu, count it on a japa mala and hear it sung — offline, with no account.
