Holika Dahan Puja

The bonfire on the eve of Holi: circling the fire with grain and thanks

Common way — most homes

Holika Dahan, Phalguna Purnima, after sunset and outside Bhadra. Muhurtha shows the window on the festival card.

About 30 minutes at the fire

Englishहिन्दीमराठीবাংলাગુજરાતીಕನ್ನಡമലയാളംଓଡ଼ିଆதமிழ்తెలుగు

On the night before Holi, a bonfire recalls how Prahlada was saved and Holika burned. Families make offerings, walk around the fire and take new grain through its warmth. Most people join a community fire; a small safe one at home is also fine. If a mantra is hard, "Om Namo Narayanaya" can stand in for any of them.

Prayers in this puja

Shubham Karoti ›Vakratunda Mahakaya ›Shuklambaradharam ›Mushikavahana ›

Get ready

A few days before

  • Find out where and when your neighbourhood's fire will be.
  • Gather dry wood and cow-dung cakes if you are helping to build it, and never green branches from living trees.

On the day

  • Keep the puja plate and water ready before sunset.
  • Check the muhurat in the app, and keep children and loose clothing well back from the fire.

The puja

  1. Light the lamp

    Light the lamp and place it to the right of the deity. Fold your hands to it.

    śubhaṁ karōti kalyāṇamārōgyaṁ dhanasampadaḥ. śatrubuddhivināśāya dīpajyōtirnamō'stu tē.I bow to the flame of the lamp, which brings good fortune, well-being, health and wealth, and destroys ill will.

    Shubham Karoti ›

  2. Remember Ganesha

    Every puja begins with Ganesha, the remover of obstacles. Fold your hands and say the verse once.

    vakratuṇḍa mahākāya sūryakōṭisamaprabha. nirvighnaṁ kuru mē dēva sarvakāryēṣu sarvadā.O Ganesha of the curved trunk and mighty form, radiant as a crore suns, keep my every work free of obstacles, always.

    Vakratunda Mahakaya ›Shuklambaradharam ›Mushikavahana ›

  3. Sankalpa — state your intention

    Hold a little water, akshat and a flower in your right palm. Say your name, today's date in the Hindu calendar, and why you are doing the puja — for example, for the well-being of the family. Then let the water fall on the ground or into a plate. Tap "Make sankalpa" to read today's full sankalpa.

  4. Offer at the unlit pile

    Before the fire is lit, sprinkle water, then offer kumkum, akshat, flowers and jaggery at the pile. Fold your hands and remember Prahlada's trust.

  5. Wind the thread

    Walk clockwise around the pile three or seven times, winding the raw cotton thread around it as you go.

  6. Light the fire

    When the muhurat arrives, the fire is lit by an elder. Stand well back and offer the coconut into it from a safe distance.

  7. Roast the grain

    Hold the wheat or barley ears near the fire on a long stick to roast them. Share the grain as prasad; it marks the new harvest.

  8. Kshama prarthana — ask forgiveness

    With folded hands, ask forgiveness for anything done wrong or left out. This closes every puja.

    āvāhanaṁ na jānāmi na jānāmi visarjanam. pūjāṁ caiva na jānāmi kṣamasva paramēśvara. mantrahīnaṁ kriyāhīnaṁ bhaktihīnaṁ surēśvara. yatpūjitaṁ mayā dēva paripūrṇaṁ tadastu mē.I do not know how to invite you, nor how to send you off, nor even how to worship; forgive me, O Lord. Whatever I have offered — lacking in mantra, in action or in devotion — may it be made whole.

  9. Take the ash home

    The next morning, when the ash is fully cold, many put a pinch on the forehead as a blessing before the colours begin.

Fasting

No fast is required. Some eat only after the fire.

Good to do

  • Keep water or sand close and watch children throughout.
  • Forgive one old grudge tonight; the fire stands for burning such things.

Tradition avoids

  • Do not burn plastic, tyres or rubbish.
  • Do not cut living trees for the fire.

The story is about trust that fire could not burn. Let the night be for letting go, and the morning for colour.

Traditions vary. Follow your own family's way.
