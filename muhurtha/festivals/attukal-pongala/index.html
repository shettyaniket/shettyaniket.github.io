When it falls

When the Moon reaches Purva Phalguni and the Sun is in Aquarius. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

The Attukal Bhagavathy temple in Thiruvananthapuram is popularly linked with Kannagi, who is said to have rested at Attukal after burning Madurai. The festival has been recorded as one of the largest gatherings of women anywhere.

How it is kept

Women cook pongala, a sweet of rice, jaggery, coconut and ghee, in earthen pots on open hearths along the city's streets, and the priest sprinkles holy water over them.
