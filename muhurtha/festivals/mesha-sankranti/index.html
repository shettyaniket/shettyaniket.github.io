When it falls

The day the Sun enters Aries. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

The Sun enters Mesha (Aries) — the solar new year. Kerala's Vishu opens with the Vishukkani, the first sight of the year, arranged the night before; Punjab's Baisakhi remembers the Khalsa's founding in 1699; Tamil Nadu's Puthandu, Bengal's Pohela Boishakh, Assam's Bohag Bihu and Odisha's Pana Sankranti all land here.

How it is kept

Vishukkani and kaineettam (coins from elders) in Kerala; bhangra and gurdwara visits for Baisakhi; new account books in Bengal; mango pachadi in Tamil Nadu.
