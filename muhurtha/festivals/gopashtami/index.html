When it falls

8th day of the waxing moon, in Kartika. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

On this day Krishna, who had looked after the calves until then, was allowed to graze the cows, and so became Gopala, protector of cows.

How it is kept

Cows are bathed, decorated with garlands and bells, worshipped and fed jaggery and green fodder, and led in procession. Goshalas hold special feeding programmes.
