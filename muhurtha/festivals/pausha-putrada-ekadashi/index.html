When it falls

11th day of the waxing moon, in Pausha. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

Significance

Pausha Putrada Ekadashi, on the bright eleventh of Pausha, is kept by couples hoping for a child — 'putrada' means the giver of sons. The katha tells of King Suketuman of Bhadravati, long childless, who kept this vow on the sages' advice and was blessed with an heir.

The story

King Suketuman of Bhadravati had no child until sages told him to keep this Ekadashi. A son was born, and the day is thought to bless couples hoping for a child.

How it is kept

Couples fast, worship Vishnu or the child Krishna and read the Putrada Ekadashi katha, breaking the fast the next day.

Fasting and parana

Devotees keep the Ekadashi fast — abstaining from grains, beans and often from food altogether, taking at most fruit, milk and water — worship Vishnu, keep a night vigil where they can, and break the fast (parana) the next morning during the Dwadashi window. The app shows the parana time for your town.

Regional and community variations

It is kept across North India in the winter month of Pausha; a second Putrada Ekadashi falls in the month of Shravana.

Foods and prasad

Sattvic phalahari food is taken; sweets and offerings are made to the child Krishna (Bal Gopal).

What to do, what to avoid

Keep the fast, worship Vishnu or the infant Krishna, read the Putrada katha, and break the fast at the Dwadashi parana; the vow is kept for the blessing of children.

Common questions

When is Pausha Putrada Ekadashi in 2027?

In 2027, Pausha Putrada Ekadashi falls on Tuesday, 19 January 2027 in Mumbai. Dates for other years are in the table above.

What is the significance of Pausha Putrada Ekadashi?

It is kept by couples wishing for children ('putrada' means giver of sons), as it blessed the childless King Suketuman with an heir.

How many Putrada Ekadashis are there?

Two — one in the winter month of Pausha and one in the monsoon month of Shravana — both kept for the blessing of children.

What are the fasting rules for Pausha Putrada Ekadashi?

Abstain from grains and beans, take fruit, milk or water, worship Vishnu or the child Krishna, and break the fast in the Dwadashi parana window.
