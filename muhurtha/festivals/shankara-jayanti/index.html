When it falls

5th day of the waxing moon, in Vaishakha. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

Adi Shankaracharya was born at Kaladi in Kerala in the eighth century. He travelled the length of India, taught Advaita Vedanta, debated scholars and founded four mathas, at Sringeri, Dwarka, Puri and Jyotirmath.

How it is kept

Followers hold lectures and recite his works such as Bhaja Govindam, and the mathas, above all Sringeri and Kaladi, hold special pujas.
