When it falls

9th day of the waxing moon, in Ashwina. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

The ninth and final night. In the South it's Ayudha Puja and Saraswati Puja — tools, books, vehicles and instruments are cleaned and worshipped.

How it is kept

Vehicles get garlands, laptops get a tilak. Fasts end with the evening aarti.
