When it falls

Full moon of Ashadha. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

In the Puranic tale, Sati was cursed by her father Daksha, whom she had offended by marrying Shiva, to spend years as a cuckoo in a garden before being reborn as Parvati and reunited with Shiva.

How it is kept

Observers traditionally keep a vow for a month from Ashadha Purnima, bathe early, worship Shiva and Gauri, give food to the needy and eat only after hearing the story.
