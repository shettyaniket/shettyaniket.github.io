When it falls

5th day of the waning moon, in Phalguna. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

Rang Panchami is the fifth day after Holika Dahan. Colours thrown in the air are believed to awaken positive forces and purify the atmosphere, and the gods themselves are said to play with colour.

How it is kept

People throw coloured powder and water. In Indore, large processions called Gair pass through the streets.
