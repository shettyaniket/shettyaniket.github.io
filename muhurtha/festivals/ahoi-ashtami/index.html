Mothers fast for their children (North India).

When it falls

8th day of the waning moon, in Ashwina. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

A folk tale tells of a woman who accidentally killed a hedgehog's cub while digging earth, and whose children then fell ill. She prayed to Ahoi Mata for forgiveness, and her children were restored.

How it is kept

Mothers fast from dawn, draw an image of Ahoi Mata on a wall and break the fast after seeing the stars. The day is kept for the safety and long life of their children.
