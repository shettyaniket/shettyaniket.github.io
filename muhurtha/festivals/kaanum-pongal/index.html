When it falls

The day after Mattu Pongal · Kanuma. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

Kaanum means 'to see', and this is the day to visit relatives and friends. In the ritual of Kanu Pidi, sisters set out food for the birds and pray for their brothers' prosperity.

How it is kept

Coloured rice balls are set out for the birds on turmeric leaves, and families go out to rivers, beaches and parks for picnics.
