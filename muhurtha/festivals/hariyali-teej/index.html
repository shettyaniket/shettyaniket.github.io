When it falls

3rd day of the waxing moon, in Shravana. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

Significance

Hariyali Teej, the green Teej, falls on the bright third of Shravana, in the heart of the monsoon. It celebrates the reunion of Parvati and Shiva, who accepted her after her long penance, and the green, rain-washed earth; women dress in green and swing on decorated swings.

The story

Parvati prayed for many years to marry Shiva, and in the rainy season he accepted her. The festival celebrates their reunion and the greenery that follows the first rains.

How it is kept

Women dress in green, apply mehndi, sing monsoon songs and swing on decorated swings hung from trees. Many keep a fast for a happy marriage.

Regional and community variations

It is kept across North India — Rajasthan, UP, Haryana, Madhya Pradesh — and Nepal; Jaipur's Teej procession, carrying an image of Parvati (Teej Mata), is famous.

Foods and prasad

Ghewar, the monsoon sweet of Rajasthan, is the signature food, along with other seasonal sweets shared among women and sent to married daughters.

What to do, what to avoid

Women wear green, apply mehndi, sing monsoon songs and swing from trees; many keep a fast for a happy married life. It is a festival of the first rains, greenery and the bond between women.

Common questions

When is Hariyali Teej in 2027?

In 2027, Hariyali Teej falls on Wednesday, 4 August 2027 in Mumbai. Dates for other years are in the table above.

What is the significance of Hariyali Teej?

It celebrates the monsoon and the reunion of Parvati and Shiva. Women keep it for marital happiness with green clothes, mehndi, swings and song.

Why is it called Hariyali Teej?

'Hariyali' means greenery — the festival falls in the monsoon, when the first rains turn the land green, and green clothes and bangles are worn to match.

What do women do on Hariyali Teej?

They dress in green, apply mehndi, swing on flower-decked swings, sing monsoon songs, and many keep a fast for a happy marriage; married daughters are sent gifts and sweets.

What sweet is eaten on Hariyali Teej?

Ghewar, the disc-shaped Rajasthani monsoon sweet soaked in syrup, is the classic Teej treat.
