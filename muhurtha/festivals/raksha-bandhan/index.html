When it falls

Full moon of Shravana. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

Raksha Bandhan (“the bond of protection”) has many origin stories — Draupadi tying a strip of her sari around Krishna's bleeding wrist is the favourite. The rakhi is a promise between siblings, by blood or by choice.

How it is kept

Sisters tie a rakhi on brothers' wrists; brothers give a gift and a promise. Tied during Shukla Purnima, ideally outside Bhadra karana — the app flags Bhadra.
