When it falls

14th day of the waxing moon, in Vaishakha. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

Significance

Narasimha Jayanti marks the appearance of Narasimha, the fierce man-lion fourth avatar of Vishnu, who burst from a pillar at dusk to kill the demon king Hiranyakashipu and save his child devotee Prahlada. He slew the demon on the threshold at twilight, outwitting a boon that made Hiranyakashipu almost impossible to kill.

The story

The demon king Hiranyakashipu had a boon that he could be killed neither by day nor night, neither indoors nor out, by neither man nor beast. At dusk Vishnu burst from a pillar as Narasimha, half man and half lion, and killed him on the threshold to protect his devoted son Prahlada.

How it is kept

Devotees fast through the day and worship Narasimha at twilight, the hour he appeared. Temples hold special abhishekam in the evening, and the story of Prahlada is read.

Fasting and parana

Devotees fast through the day and worship Narasimha at Sayankal, the twilight hour of his appearance; the fast is broken after the evening puja. The story of Prahlada is read.

Regional and community variations

It is kept across the Vaishnava world, with special devotion in Andhra, Telangana and Karnataka; Ahobilam in Andhra, the great Narasimha kshetra, and temples such as Melkote hold grand abhishekam.

Foods and prasad

Cooling offerings suited to the fierce deity and the summer season — panakam, kosambari, buttermilk and sweet prasad — are shared after the twilight worship.

What to do, what to avoid

The worship is timed to twilight, the hour Narasimha appeared, neither day nor night; keep the fast until then and read the Prahlada story, which teaches that devotion is protected whatever the odds.

Common questions

When is Narasimha Jayanti in 2027?

In 2027, Narasimha Jayanti falls on Tuesday, 18 May 2027 in Mumbai. Dates for other years are in the table above.

What is the significance of Narasimha Jayanti?

It celebrates Vishnu's appearance as Narasimha, the man-lion, who killed the demon Hiranyakashipu to protect his devotee Prahlada — a sign that God protects devotion against any power.

Why did Vishnu take the form of Narasimha?

Hiranyakashipu had a boon that he could not be killed by man or beast, by day or night, indoors or out. Vishnu came as Narasimha — neither man nor beast — at dusk, on the threshold, and killed him, honouring the boon's letter while defeating him.

When is Narasimha worshipped on his Jayanti?

At Sayankal, the twilight hour of his appearance; devotees fast through the day and worship him in the evening.

Which is the most famous Narasimha temple?

Ahobilam in Andhra Pradesh, with its nine shrines of Narasimha, is the foremost; Narasimha is widely worshipped across the South and the Vaishnava world.
