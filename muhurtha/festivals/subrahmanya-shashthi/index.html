When it falls

6th day of the waxing moon, in Margashirsha. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

Subrahmanya Shashthi is the sixth day of the waxing moon in Margashirsha, sacred to Subrahmanya, the Tamil Murugan and Kartikeya. At Kukke Subrahmanya in Dakshina Kannada it is the day of the great car festival, and the temple is a centre of serpent worship. Maharashtra keeps the same tithi for Khandoba as Champa Shashthi.

How it is kept

Devotees fast or eat simply, bathe in the Kumaradhara river at Kukke, offer milk and fruit at the temple or at a serpent stone, and watch the car procession.
