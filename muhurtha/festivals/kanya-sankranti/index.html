When it falls

The day the Sun enters Virgo. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

Sankranti is the day the Sun moves from one zodiac sign into the next, here from Simha into Kanya, and a new solar month begins. Many homes keep it as a day for a bath, a gift and a quiet prayer to the Sun.

How it is kept

Bathe early, offer water to the Sun at sunrise, and give something, such as grain, sesame or a meal, to someone in need. The regional solar calendars begin their new month today.
