The story

Koral Parba, also called Kadiru Habba or Thene Habba, welcomes the first rice of the new harvest. Families in Tulu Nadu cut the first sheaves of paddy, bring them home and offer them before anyone eats from the new crop. The date varies by family and by locality, so check a Tulu panchanga for yours.

How it is kept

The men of the house go to the field early, pray to the crop and cut an odd number of ears of paddy: three, five, seven or nine. The ears are brought home and offered to the household deity, and a meal made with the new rice follows.
