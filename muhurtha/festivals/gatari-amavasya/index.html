When it falls

New moon of Ashadha. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

Gatari is the last new moon before Shravan, a month in which many in Maharashtra give up meat and fish. It is kept as a day of feasting before the fast, and lamps are cleaned and worshipped as Dipa Pooja.

How it is kept

Homes clean and light every lamp, worship them with flowers and sweets, and families eat a non-vegetarian meal before the vegetarian month begins.
