When it falls

Full moon of Ashwina. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

The autumn full moon, when the Moon is said to shower amrit. Krishna danced the raas with the gopis on this night.

How it is kept

Kheer is left out under moonlight and eaten in the morning. Kojagiri — “who is awake?” — Lakshmi visits those who stay up.
