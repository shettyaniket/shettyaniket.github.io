When it falls

14th day of the waxing moon, in Bhadrapada. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

Significance

Anant Chaturdashi is the day Vishnu is worshipped as Ananta, the endless one, and a sacred thread of fourteen knots (the Anant Sutra) is tied on the wrist for protection and prosperity. It is also the last and biggest day of the ten-day Ganesh festival, when the great immersions take place.

The story

Vishnu is worshipped as Anant — endless — with a fourteen-knot thread tied on the wrist. It's also the last day of the Ganesh festival, when the big immersions happen.

How it is kept

Anant Sutra puja at home; in Mumbai, the final Ganpati processions to the sea.

Fasting and parana

Those keeping the Ananta vrat fast, worship Vishnu, tie the fourteen-knot thread, and read the vrat katha; the vow is traditionally kept for fourteen years. The thread is worn until it is replaced the next year.

Regional and community variations

The Ananta vrat is kept especially in Maharashtra, Gujarat, Odisha and the South; in Mumbai and Pune the day is dominated by the final, vast Ganpati visarjan processions to the sea and rivers.

Foods and prasad

A sweet of fourteen items or a simple sattvic meal is offered to Vishnu; wheat-flour sweets are common in the Ananta vrat.

What to do, what to avoid

On the Ganesh side of the day, keep immersions responsible — eco idols and controlled immersion spare the waters; on the Vishnu side, keep the vrat's simplicity and charity.

Common questions

When is Anant Chaturdashi in 2027?

In 2027, Anant Chaturdashi falls on Tuesday, 14 September 2027 in Mumbai. Dates for other years are in the table above.

What is the significance of Anant Chaturdashi?

It honours Vishnu as Ananta, the infinite, with a fourteen-knot protective thread and a vrat for prosperity; it is also the final day of the ten-day Ganesh festival.

What is the Anant Sutra?

A sacred thread of fourteen knots, dyed and knotted, tied on the wrist on Anant Chaturdashi after the worship of Vishnu, and worn through the year for protection.

Why is Ganpati visarjan done on Anant Chaturdashi?

The ten-day Ganesh festival that begins on Ganesh Chaturthi ends on Anant Chaturdashi, when the idols are carried in procession and immersed with the farewell 'Ganpati bappa morya'.

How long is the Ananta vrat kept?

Traditionally the vow is taken for fourteen years, with the fourteen-knot thread renewed each Anant Chaturdashi.
