When it falls

The day before Makar Sankranti · Pongal. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

Bhogi is the first day of the Pongal festival, named for Indra, lord of the rains, who is thanked for the harvest. The old is cleared away so the new year can begin.

How it is kept

Before dawn, old furniture and worn household goods are burned in a bonfire. Homes are cleaned, kolams are drawn, and in Andhra children are bathed with sweet fruits (Bhogi pallu). The first Pongal preparations follow.
