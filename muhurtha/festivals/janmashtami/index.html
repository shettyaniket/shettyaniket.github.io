When it falls

8th day of the waning moon, in Shravana. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

Significance

Krishna Janmashtami marks the birth of Krishna, the eighth avatar of Vishnu, born at midnight in a Mathura prison to end the tyranny of his uncle Kansa. It is one of the most loved festivals of the Vaishnava world, kept with fasting, song and the night-long wait for the midnight hour of his birth.

The story

Krishna was born in Mathura's prison at midnight on Shravana's dark eighth, in Rohini nakshatra, and smuggled across the Yamuna to Gokul. The midnight rule for this festival comes straight from the story.

Mathura, where Krishna was born, and nearby Vrindavan, where he grew up, hold the largest celebrations, along with the ISKCON temples and the shrines at Dwarka and Nathdwara.

How it is kept

Fast until midnight, then bathe and dress the infant Krishna (bal gopal) in a cradle. The next day is Dahi Handi — human pyramids breaking a pot of curd, as the young Krishna stole butter.

Devotees fast through the day and break it only after the midnight birth, once the Ashtami tithi is current. The infant is bathed with milk, curd, honey and water, dressed, rocked in a flower-decked cradle and offered the chhappan bhog, fifty-six dishes. In Maharashtra the next morning is Gokulashtami, when govinda troupes build human pyramids to reach and break a high pot of curd.

Fasting and parana

Devotees fast through the day — some without water — and break it (parana) only after the midnight birth, once the Ashtami tithi, and by stricter rules Rohini nakshatra, are present. Vrat food of fruit, milk and sabudana is taken; the fast is broken with the midnight prasad of the infant Krishna.

Regional and community variations

Mathura and Vrindavan, Krishna's birthplace and childhood home, hold the largest celebrations, with the ISKCON temples, Dwarka and Nathdwara close behind. In Maharashtra the next morning is Dahi Handi (Gokulashtami), when govinda troupes build human pyramids to break a high pot of curd; the South keeps it as Gokulashtami or Sri Krishna Jayanti with kolam and butter offerings.

Foods and prasad

The infant Krishna is offered the chhappan bhog, fifty-six dishes, and butter, curd, makhan-mishri, panjiri and sweets, reflecting his childhood love of butter and curd.

What to do, what to avoid

Keep the cradle and the altar clean and the vigil until midnight if you can; those who cannot stay up do an evening puja. Avoid grain and heavy food until the fast is broken after the birth.

Common questions

When is Krishna Janmashtami in 2027?

In 2027, Krishna Janmashtami falls on Tuesday, 24 August 2027 in Mumbai. Dates for other years are in the table above.

What is the significance of Krishna Janmashtami?

It celebrates the birth of Krishna, the eighth avatar of Vishnu, who was born to destroy the tyrant Kansa and to teach the Bhagavad Gita. For devotees it is the appearance of God himself in human form.

Why is Janmashtami celebrated at midnight?

Because Krishna was born at midnight, in Rohini nakshatra, in the prison of Mathura. The puja and the breaking of the fast are therefore timed to the midnight hour.

What are the fasting rules for Janmashtami?

Devotees fast through the day, some on fruit and milk and some without water, and break it only after the midnight birth, once the Ashtami tithi is current. Grain is avoided until the parana.

What is Dahi Handi?

The morning after Janmashtami in Maharashtra, young men (govindas) build human pyramids to reach and break a pot of curd hung high above the street — a re-enactment of the child Krishna stealing butter.

What food is offered on Janmashtami?

The chhappan bhog, fifty-six dishes, along with butter, makhan-mishri, curd, panjiri and sweets — foods tied to Krishna's childhood love of butter.

Which places celebrate Janmashtami the most?

Mathura and Vrindavan above all, then the ISKCON temples, Dwarka and Nathdwara; Maharashtra is known for Dahi Handi.
