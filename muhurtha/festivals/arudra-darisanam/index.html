When it falls

When the Moon reaches Ardra and the Sun is in Sagittarius. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

Arudra Darisanam is the day Shiva is worshipped as Nataraja, the lord of the cosmic dance, on the Ardra (Thiruvathirai) nakshatra in the month of Margazhi. Its most famous celebration is at the Nataraja temple in Chidambaram. Kerala keeps the same star as Thiruvathira, with women's dances.

How it is kept

Devotees fast and keep a night vigil, then visit a Shiva temple at dawn, where the deity is bathed and taken out in procession. Thiruvathirai kali, a sweet of rice and jaggery, is offered, often with a stew of seven vegetables.
