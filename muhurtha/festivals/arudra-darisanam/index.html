Significance

Arudra Darisanam is the day Shiva is worshipped as Nataraja, the lord of the cosmic dance whose Ananda Tandava sets the universe in motion. It falls on the Ardra (Thiruvathirai) star in Margazhi, the fullest expression of Shiva's grace, and its most famous celebration is at the Nataraja temple in Chidambaram.

The story

Arudra Darisanam is the day Shiva is worshipped as Nataraja, the lord of the cosmic dance, on the Ardra (Thiruvathirai) nakshatra in the month of Margazhi. Its most famous celebration is at the Nataraja temple in Chidambaram. Kerala keeps the same star as Thiruvathira, with women's dances.

How it is kept

Devotees fast and keep a night vigil, then visit a Shiva temple at dawn, where the deity is bathed and taken out in procession. Thiruvathirai kali, a sweet of rice and jaggery, is offered, often with a stew of seven vegetables.

Fasting and parana

Devotees fast and keep a night vigil, then take darshan of Nataraja at dawn, when the deity is bathed and taken in procession; Kerala's Thiruvathira is kept with a fast and the kali dance.

Regional and community variations

Chidambaram holds the grandest Arudra Darisanam, with the abhishekam of Nataraja; Kerala keeps the same star as Thiruvathira, with women's Thiruvathirakali dances and a day-long fast.

Foods and prasad

Thiruvathirai kali, a sweet of rice and jaggery, is offered with a stew of seven vegetables (ezhu kari kootu); in Kerala, koova and seasonal dishes mark the day.

What to do, what to avoid

Keep the vigil and the dawn darshan of Nataraja, offer the kali and seven-vegetable stew, and reflect on the cosmic dance of creation and dissolution.

Common questions

When is Arudra Darisanam · Thiruvathira in 2026?

In 2026, Arudra Darisanam · Thiruvathira falls on Thursday, 24 December 2026 in Mumbai. Dates for other years are in the table above.

What is the significance of Arudra Darisanam?

It is the day Shiva is worshipped as Nataraja, the lord of the cosmic dance, on the Ardra star in Margazhi — a celebration of divine grace, grandest at Chidambaram.

Who is Nataraja?

Shiva in his form as the cosmic dancer, whose Ananda Tandava embodies the rhythm of creation, preservation and dissolution; the Chidambaram temple enshrines this form.

What is Thiruvathira?

The Kerala observance on the same Ardra star, kept by women with a fast and the Thiruvathirakali dance around a lamp, for marital happiness and long life.
