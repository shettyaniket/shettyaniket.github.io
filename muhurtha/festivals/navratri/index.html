When it falls

1st day of the waxing moon, in Ashwina. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

Significance

Sharad Navratri is nine nights for the Goddess, ending in Dussehra. It recalls Durga's nine-day battle with the buffalo-demon Mahishasura, whom she slew on the tenth day, and Rama's worship of the Goddess before marching on Lanka. It is the best known of the year's four Navratris.

The story

Nine nights for the Goddess in her nine forms, ending with Dussehra. Durga fought Mahishasura for nine days; Rama worshipped her before marching on Lanka.

The nine nights honour nine forms of Durga, one to a night — Shailaputri, Brahmacharini, Chandraghanta, Kushmanda, Skandamata, Katyayani, Kalaratri, Mahagauri and Siddhidatri. This autumn Navratri is the best known of the year's four; the spring one, Chaitra Navratri, ends with Ram Navami.

How it is kept

Ghatasthapana — a pot of sprouting barley — starts it. Gujarat dances garba every night; Bengal builds pandals; the South arranges golu dolls; many people fast or eat sattvic food.

Ashtami and Navami bring Kanya Puja, when young girls are honoured as the Goddess and fed puri, chana and halwa. Those who fast keep a sattvic vrat diet — fruit, kuttu and singhara flour, sabudana and sendha namak — and set aside grains, onion and garlic. In the South the Bommai Golu display of dolls is arranged in tiers, and the tenth day is Dussehra.

Fasting and parana

Many keep a fast for all nine days, either eating one sattvic meal or living on vrat foods — fruit, milk, kuttu (buckwheat) and singhara (water-chestnut) flour, sabudana and sendha namak — and setting aside grain, onion and garlic. The eighth day (Ashtami) or ninth (Navami) brings Kanya Puja, when young girls are honoured as the Goddess and fed, and most people break the nine-day fast after it.

Regional and community variations

Gujarat dances garba and dandiya-raas around a lamp every night; Bengal and the East build pandals and keep the last four days as Durga Puja; Tamil Nadu and the South arrange the Bommai Golu, a tiered display of dolls, and exchange visits; the North stages Ramlila plays each evening, ending with the burning of Ravana on Dussehra.

Foods and prasad

Vrat kitchens turn out sabudana khichdi and vada, kuttu puris, singhare ki poori, samak rice and fruit; the Kanya Puja feast is puri, kala chana and sooji halwa. In Gujarat the fast is lighter so people can dance through the night.

What to do, what to avoid

During the fast, grain, lentils, onion, garlic and non-vegetarian food are set aside; many also avoid alcohol, haircuts and new ventures until the tenth day. Keep the Ghatasthapana pot and the lamp undisturbed for the nine days.

Common questions

When is Sharad Navratri begins in 2027?

In 2027, Sharad Navratri begins falls on Thursday, 30 September 2027 in Mumbai. Dates for other years are in the table above.

What is the significance of Navratri?

Navratri honours the Goddess Durga over nine nights, recalling her victory over the demon Mahishasura. Each night is given to one of her nine forms, and the tenth day, Dussehra, celebrates the triumph of good over evil.

Who are the nine forms of Durga worshipped in Navratri?

Shailaputri, Brahmacharini, Chandraghanta, Kushmanda, Skandamata, Katyayani, Kalaratri, Mahagauri and Siddhidatri — one for each of the nine nights.

What can you eat during Navratri fasting?

Vrat food: fruit and milk, kuttu and singhara flour, sabudana, samak rice, potatoes and sendha namak (rock salt). Grain, lentils, onion, garlic and non-vegetarian food are avoided.

What is Kanya Puja or Kanjak?

On Ashtami or Navami, nine (or more) young girls are honoured as living forms of the Goddess, their feet washed, and fed puri, kala chana and halwa. Most people break the nine-day fast after this.

How is Navratri celebrated in different states?

Gujarat dances garba and dandiya, Bengal keeps Durga Puja with grand pandals, Tamil Nadu arranges the Golu doll display, and the North stages Ramlila ending with Dussehra.

How is the date of Navratri decided?

Sharad Navratri begins on the first day of the bright fortnight of Ashwina and runs nine nights. The Hindu calendar is lunisolar, so the Gregorian date shifts each year; Muhurtha works it out from the Sun and Moon.
