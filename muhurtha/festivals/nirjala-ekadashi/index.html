Nirjala Ekadashi

The strictest Ekadashi — kept without even water; Bhima's Ekadashi.

When it falls

11th day of the waxing moon, in Jyeshtha. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

Bhima, the strongest of the Pandavas, could not bear to fast on Ekadashi. The sage Vyasa told him that keeping this one waterless Ekadashi in Jyeshtha would earn the merit of all twenty-four, so it is also called Bhimaseni Ekadashi.

How it is kept

Devotees fast without food or water from sunrise to the next sunrise, worship Vishnu, give water pots, fans and fruit in charity, and break the fast the next morning.
