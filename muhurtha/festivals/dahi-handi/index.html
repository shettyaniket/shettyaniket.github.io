When it falls

The day after Krishna Janmashtami. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

Significance

Dahi Handi, kept the day after Janmashtami as Gokulashtami in Maharashtra, re-enacts the child Krishna's love of butter and curd. As a boy he and his friends formed human towers to reach the pots that mothers hung high out of reach; today teams of young men do the same, climbing to break a curd pot strung above the street.

The story

As a child Krishna loved butter and curd, and with his friends he formed a human tower to steal the pots mothers hung out of reach. Dahi Handi recreates this on the day after Janmashtami.

How it is kept

Teams of young men, called Govindas, form human pyramids to reach a pot of curd hung high above the street and break it. Mumbai, Thane and Pune are known for it.

Regional and community variations

It is the great Janmashtami-season spectacle of Maharashtra — Mumbai, Thane and Pune — where govinda troupes (mandals) compete to build the tallest pyramids for prize pots hung storeys high; the pots and prizes grow bigger every year.

Foods and prasad

The broken pot's curd and butter are shared; the day is festive with Krishna's favourite dairy foods.

What to do, what to avoid

The pyramids are a feat of teamwork and courage; safety harnesses, padding and age limits now protect the young govindas who climb. It is a joyful, competitive celebration of Krishna's boyhood.

Common questions

When is Dahi Handi in 2027?

In 2027, Dahi Handi falls on Wednesday, 25 August 2027 in Mumbai. Dates for other years are in the table above.

What is the significance of Dahi Handi?

It re-enacts the child Krishna stealing butter and curd by forming human towers; kept the day after Janmashtami, it celebrates his playful boyhood in Gokul.

Who are the Govindas in Dahi Handi?

The teams of young men who build human pyramids to reach and break the curd pot hung high above the street, named for Govinda, a name of Krishna.

Where is Dahi Handi celebrated the most?

In Maharashtra — Mumbai, Thane and Pune — where mandals compete for prize pots strung high between buildings.

When is Dahi Handi held?

On the day after Krishna Janmashtami, kept as Gokulashtami in Maharashtra.
