When it falls

2nd day of the waxing moon, in Chaitra. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

Significance

Cheti Chand is the Sindhi new year and the birthday of Jhulelal (Uderolal), the patron saint and ishta deva of the Sindhi community, a form of Varuna, the water god. Sindhi tradition says he appeared to protect the people of Sindh when a ruler threatened to force them to give up their faith.

The story

According to Sindhi tradition, a ruler in Sindh threatened to force people to give up their faith, and they prayed to the river god. Jhulelal appeared and protected them. His birth is celebrated as the Sindhi new year.

How it is kept

Sindhi families visit Jhulelal temples, carry a lamp and water pot in procession and share sweet dishes. The day is also a time to wear new clothes and visit relatives.

Regional and community variations

It is kept by Sindhis across India and the world, falling on the second day of the bright fortnight of Chaitra, near Ugadi and Gudi Padwa; Sindhi temples (Tikanas) hold the main celebrations.

Foods and prasad

Sweet dishes such as tahiri (sweet rice) and sai bhaji are shared; the Baharana Sahib — a lamp, water pot, sweets and coconut — is offered to Jhulelal.

What to do, what to avoid

Visit the Jhulelal temple, carry the Baharana Sahib in procession to the water, wear new clothes and share sweets; it is a joyful new-year celebration of community and faith.

Common questions

When is Cheti Chand in 2027?

In 2027, Cheti Chand falls on Thursday, 8 April 2027 in Mumbai. Dates for other years are in the table above.

What is the significance of Cheti Chand?

It is the Sindhi new year and the birthday of Jhulelal, the community's patron saint and a form of the water god Varuna, who is said to have appeared to protect the people of Sindh.

Who is Jhulelal?

The patron deity of the Sindhi community (Uderolal), revered as an incarnation of Varuna, the god of water, who protected the Sindhis' faith; Cheti Chand is his appearance day.

How is Cheti Chand celebrated?

Sindhi families visit Jhulelal temples, carry the Baharana Sahib in procession to a river or the sea, wear new clothes, and share sweets to mark the new year.
