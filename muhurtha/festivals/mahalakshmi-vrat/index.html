When it falls

8th day of the waxing moon, in Bhadrapada. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

A sixteen-day vow to Mahalakshmi, also worshipped as Gajalakshmi, begins on Radha Ashtami and ends on the eighth day of the dark fortnight. It is kept for prosperity and the family's wellbeing.

How it is kept

Women tie a thread with sixteen knots on the arm, worship a pot that stands for Lakshmi, and on the last day hold a large puja with an elephant image and feed those in need.
