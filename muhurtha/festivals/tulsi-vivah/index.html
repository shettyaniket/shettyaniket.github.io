When it falls

12th day of the waxing moon, in Kartika. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

Significance

Tulsi Vivah is the ceremonial marriage of the tulsi (holy basil) plant — the devotee Vrinda reborn as a plant — to Vishnu, in the form of a shaligram stone or a Krishna image. It closes Chaturmas, the four months when Vishnu is said to sleep and weddings are paused, and so opens the Hindu wedding season.

The story

The tulsi plant — Vrinda, a devotee reborn — is ceremonially married to Vishnu (as a shaligram). It closes Chaturmas and opens the wedding season.

How it is kept

A miniature wedding in the courtyard: mandap, mangalsutra on the plant, sugarcane and amla.

Regional and community variations

It is kept across North and West India, especially Maharashtra and the Vaishnava traditions, over the days from Prabodhini Ekadashi to Kartik Purnima; the courtyard tulsi is dressed as a bride and married with full ritual.

Foods and prasad

Sugarcane, amla (gooseberry), tamarind and the season's new produce are offered, along with sweets; a small wedding feast is shared.

What to do, what to avoid

The tulsi is given a mandap, a sari and a mangalsutra and married at dusk like a daughter; it is an auspicious, joyful rite that marks the return of the wedding season.

Common questions

When is Tulsi Vivah in 2026?

In 2026, Tulsi Vivah falls on Saturday, 21 November 2026 in Mumbai. Dates for other years are in the table above.

What is the significance of Tulsi Vivah?

It is the marriage of the sacred tulsi plant to Vishnu, which closes the four-month Chaturmas and opens the Hindu wedding season. It honours the devotee Vrinda and the tulsi's place in Vaishnava worship.

When does the wedding season begin after Tulsi Vivah?

Tulsi Vivah ends Chaturmas, the period from Devshayani to Prabodhini Ekadashi when weddings and new ventures are paused; after it, auspicious wedding dates resume.

How is Tulsi Vivah performed?

The courtyard tulsi plant is dressed as a bride with a sari and ornaments, given a small mandap, and married at dusk to a shaligram or Krishna image with the rites of a Hindu wedding.

What is offered at Tulsi Vivah?

Sugarcane, amla, tamarind and seasonal produce, along with sweets, placed around the tulsi during the ceremony.
