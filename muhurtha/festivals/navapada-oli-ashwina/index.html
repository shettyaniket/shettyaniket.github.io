When it falls

7th day of the waxing moon, in Ashwina. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

Navapada Oli is a nine-day Jain observance, kept twice a year, in Ashwina and Chaitra, from the bright seventh to the full moon. It honours the nine padas: the Arihants, Siddhas, Acharyas, Upadhyayas, Sadhus, and right faith, knowledge, conduct and austerity.

How it is kept

Many keep ayambil, a single plain meal a day without oil, salt, spices, milk or sweets, and spend the days in worship and study. Practices differ by community, so follow your own tradition.
