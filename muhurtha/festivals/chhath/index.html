Significance

Chhath Puja is an ancient festival of the Sun, kept over four days in Bihar, Jharkhand, eastern UP and the Terai of Nepal. It thanks Surya and Chhathi Maiya (Usha, the dawn) for life and sustenance, and is kept with extraordinary discipline — a 36-hour waterless fast and offerings to the setting and then the rising Sun, standing in the river.

The story

An ancient sun festival from Bihar — four days of purity ending with offerings to the setting and then the rising Sun, standing in the river.

How it is kept

Thekua sweets, sugarcane, and a 36-hour waterless fast for the devotee (the vrati). No priests, no idols — just the Sun and the water.

Fasting and parana

The four days are Nahay Khay (bathing and a pure meal), Kharna (a day-long fast broken at dusk with kheer, then the start of the 36-hour waterless fast), Sandhya Arghya (offering to the setting Sun), and Usha Arghya (offering to the rising Sun, which ends the fast). The vrati takes no food or water for the thirty-six hours between Kharna and the final arghya.

Regional and community variations

It is the signature festival of Bihar, Jharkhand, eastern Uttar Pradesh and Mithila in Nepal, now kept by migrants in Mumbai, Delhi and across the world. It has no priests and no idols — only the Sun, the water and the devotee (vrati).

Foods and prasad

Thekua (a wheat-and-jaggery biscuit), rice-flour laddoos, sugarcane, coconut and seasonal fruit are prepared with great purity and carried to the ghat in bamboo baskets (soop and daura) as prasad.

What to do, what to avoid

Everything is kept scrupulously clean and pure; the prasad is cooked on a fresh clay stove over mango wood, and the devotee follows strict rules of chastity and simplicity. The waterless fast is severe and kept only by those able to.

Common questions

When is Chhath Puja in 2026?

In 2026, Chhath Puja falls on Sunday, 15 November 2026 in Mumbai. Dates for other years are in the table above.

What is the significance of Chhath Puja?

It thanks the Sun god, Surya, and Chhathi Maiya for life, health and prosperity. It is a festival of purity, gratitude and endurance, with no priests or idols — only the Sun and the water.

What are the four days of Chhath?

Nahay Khay (bathing and a pure meal), Kharna (a fast broken at dusk with kheer), Sandhya Arghya (offering to the setting Sun), and Usha Arghya (offering to the rising Sun, which ends the fast).

What are the fasting rules for Chhath?

The main vrati keeps a 36-hour nirjala fast — without food or water — from the evening of Kharna until the Usha Arghya to the rising Sun on the final morning.

Why is arghya offered to both the setting and the rising Sun?

Chhath honours the whole cycle of the Sun — thanking it at dusk for the day given, and greeting it at dawn for the day to come; the setting-Sun offering is as revered as the rising one.

What is the prasad of Chhath Puja?

Thekua, a wheat-flour and jaggery sweet, with rice laddoos, sugarcane, coconut and seasonal fruit, all prepared under strict purity and offered at the river ghat.
