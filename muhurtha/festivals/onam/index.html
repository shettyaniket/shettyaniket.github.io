When it falls

When the Moon reaches Shravana and the Sun is in Leo. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

Significance

Onam is the great harvest festival of Kerala, kept over ten days and welcoming the just asura king Mahabali, who by a boon of Vishnu's Vamana avatar returns to visit his people once a year. It is a festival of plenty, equality and homecoming, kept by Keralites of every faith.

The story

Mahabali, a just asura king, was sent to the netherworld by Vishnu's Vamana avatar but allowed to visit his people once a year. Kerala welcomes him for ten days ending on Thiruvonam.

How it is kept

Pookalam flower carpets, the Onam sadya of two dozen dishes on a banana leaf, snake-boat races, and Pulikali tiger dances.

The ten days open on Atham and build to Thiruvonam, the pookalam flower carpet growing wider each day, with clay cones of Thrikkakara Appan — Vishnu as Vamana — set at the centre of the home. The Aranmula and Nehru Trophy snake-boat races draw huge crowds, and families put on new Onakkodi clothes for the sadya.

Regional and community variations

Onam is Kerala's own, but is kept wherever Malayalis live. The ten days run from Atham to Thiruvonam, the main day; nearby Tamil regions and the Tulu coast share some of the harvest spirit.

Foods and prasad

The Onam sadya is a vegetarian feast of two dozen or more dishes served on a banana leaf — rice, sambar, avial, thoran, olan, pachadi, pappadam, banana chips and the payasam that ends the meal.

What to do, what to avoid

The days build from Atham, the pookalam (flower carpet) growing wider each day, with clay cones of Thrikkakara Appan — Vishnu as Vamana — set at the centre of the home. The Aranmula and Nehru Trophy snake-boat races (vallamkali) and Pulikali tiger dances are the great public events, and families wear new Onakkodi clothes for the sadya.

Common questions

When is Onam · Thiruvonam in 2027?

In 2027, Onam · Thiruvonam falls on Sunday, 12 September 2027 in Mumbai. Dates for other years are in the table above.

What is the significance of Onam?

Onam is Kerala's harvest festival and the annual homecoming of King Mahabali, whose reign is remembered as a golden age of equality and plenty. It celebrates abundance, unity and the welcoming of a beloved king.

What is the story of Onam?

Mahabali, a just and generous asura king, grew so powerful that the gods grew uneasy. Vishnu came as the dwarf Vamana, asked for three paces of land, and in three steps covered the worlds; he sent Mahabali to the netherworld but granted him one visit to his people each year — which is Onam.

What is an Onam sadya?

A grand vegetarian feast of two dozen or more dishes served on a banana leaf — rice, sambar, avial, thoran, olan, pachadi, pickles, pappadam and payasam — eaten on Thiruvonam.

How long does Onam last?

Ten days, from Atham to Thiruvonam, the main day, with the pookalam flower carpet growing larger each day.

What is a pookalam?

The flower carpet (pookalam) laid at the entrance of the home during Onam, begun on Atham and made larger and more intricate each day until Thiruvonam.

What are the famous Onam events?

The Aranmula and Nehru Trophy snake-boat races (vallamkali) and the Pulikali tiger dances are the best-known public celebrations.
