When it falls

14th day of the waning moon, in Magha. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

The great night of Shiva: the night he married Parvati, or the night he danced the tandava, or the night the linga appeared as a pillar of light. Kept at midnight, in the darkest fortnight of the coldest month.

How it is kept

Fasting, a night vigil with four rounds of abhishek (milk, curd, honey, water), bel leaves, and the chant Om Namah Shivaya. Devotees who can't stay up do one evening round.

The night runs in four prahars, each with its own abhishek and an offering of bel (bael) leaves, which Shiva is said to love, along with datura and, where it is the custom, bhang. The twelve Jyotirlinga shrines — Somnath, Mahakaleshwar at Ujjain, Kashi Vishwanath and the rest — keep their biggest night of the year, and Mandi in Himachal gathers its village deities for a week-long fair.
