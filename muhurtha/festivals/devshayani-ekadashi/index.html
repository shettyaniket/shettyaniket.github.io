Devshayani (Ashadhi) Ekadashi

Vishnu begins his four-month sleep and Chaturmas starts; the Pandharpur Wari reaches Vitthal.

When it falls

11th day of the waxing moon, in Ashadha. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

On this day Vishnu is said to go to sleep on the serpent Shesha in the cosmic ocean for four months, until Prabodhini Ekadashi. These months, Chaturmas, are kept quiet and devout, and weddings are not held.

How it is kept

Devotees fast and worship Vishnu. Lakhs of Varkaris walk to Pandharpur in Maharashtra with palanquins of Sant Dnyaneshwar and Sant Tukaram to worship Vithoba.
