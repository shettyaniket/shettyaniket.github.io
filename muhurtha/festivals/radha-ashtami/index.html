When it falls

8th day of the waxing moon, in Bhadrapada. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

Radha is Krishna's beloved and, in Vaishnava tradition, his inseparable energy. Her birthday falls on the bright eighth of Bhadrapada, about two weeks after Krishna's.

How it is kept

Devotees fast, worship Radha and Krishna together and sing bhajans. Barsana and Vrindavan in the Braj region hold the main celebrations.
