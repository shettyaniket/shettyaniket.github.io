When it falls

The day after Chhath Puja. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

On the fourth morning devotees stand in water at dawn and offer arghya to the rising Sun, which completes the fast.

How it is kept

Families gather at river ghats before sunrise with bamboo baskets of fruit and thekua, offer water and milk to the Sun and then break the fast with prasad.
