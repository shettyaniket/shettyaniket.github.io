In 2027, the big days are Holi on 22 March, Dussehra on 9 October and Diwali on 29 October. All 161 festivals, fasts and observances are below, worked out from the Moon and the Sun. Dates are for Mumbai; a festival can fall a day apart elsewhere, because tithis change at different local times.
