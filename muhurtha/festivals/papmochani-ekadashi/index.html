When it falls

11th day of the waning moon, in Phalguna. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

Papmochani means 'remover of sins'. The katha tells of the sage Medhavi, whose penance was broken by the apsara Manjughosha. On Vishnu's advice he kept this Ekadashi and was freed of the sin.

How it is kept

Devotees fast, worship Vishnu with tulsi and read the katha. It is the last Ekadashi of the lunar year, before Chaitra begins.
