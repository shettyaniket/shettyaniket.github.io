When it falls

2nd day of the waning moon, in Phalguna. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

Sant Tukaram, the 17th-century Varkari poet of Dehu, wrote thousands of Marathi abhangas in praise of Vitthal. Tradition says he was taken to Vaikuntha in a chariot on this day.

How it is kept

Varkaris gather at Dehu near Pune, hold kirtans and recite his abhangas, and a procession moves along the Indrayani river.
