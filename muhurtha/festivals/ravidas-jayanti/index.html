When it falls

Full moon of Magha. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

Guru Ravidas, the saint of Varanasi in the 15th and 16th centuries, was a cobbler by trade and taught that devotion matters more than caste. Some of his verses are included in the Guru Granth Sahib.

How it is kept

Followers hold processions with hymn-singing (Nagar Kirtan) and visit the Guru Ravidas Janam Asthan in Varanasi, and many bathe in the Ganga.
