Rishi Panchami

Honouring the seven sages; kept after Hartalika Teej.

Dates are for Mumbai. A tithi that changes close to sunrise or sunset can fall a day earlier or later in another city, so for your own town open the festival in the app.

When it falls

5th day of the waxing moon, in Bhadrapada. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

The seven sages, the Saptarishi, are honoured on this day: Kashyapa, Atri, Bharadvaja, Vishvamitra, Gautama, Jamadagni and Vasishtha. It is kept to atone for unintended lapses in purity rules.

How it is kept

Women bathe in a river and fast, eating a single meal of foods grown without a plough, and worship the seven sages.
