When it falls

When the Moon reaches Pushya and the Sun is in Capricorn. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

Significance

Thaipusam falls when the Moon is in the Pusam (Pushya) star in the Tamil month of Thai. It marks the day Parvati gave her son Murugan the vel, the divine spear with which he destroyed the demon Surapadman — a victory of light and virtue over darkness. Devotees carry the kavadi as an act of penance and thanksgiving.

The story

On this day Parvati is said to have given her son Murugan the vel, the spear with which he defeated the demon Surapadman. The festival falls on the Pusam star in the month of Thai.

How it is kept

Devotees carry kavadi, decorated frames, on their shoulders to Murugan temples such as Palani. In Malaysia and Singapore, some pierce their skin with skewers as a vow.

Regional and community variations

It is the great Murugan festival of Tamil communities — Palani, Tiruchendur, Swamimalai and the other Arupadai Veedu temples in Tamil Nadu, and spectacularly at Batu Caves in Malaysia and in Singapore, where huge kavadi processions are held.

Foods and prasad

Milk, panchamirtham (Palani's famous five-fruit preserve) and vegetarian offerings are made; devotees keep a sattvic diet during their vow.

What to do, what to avoid

Those who take the vow keep a period of fasting, celibacy and simple living before carrying the kavadi; some pierce the skin or tongue with small vels and hooks as an act of penance, borne without pain through devotion.

Common questions

When is Thaipusam in 2027?

In 2027, Thaipusam falls on Saturday, 23 January 2027 in Mumbai. Dates for other years are in the table above.

What is the significance of Thaipusam?

It celebrates Murugan receiving the vel from his mother Parvati to defeat the demon Surapadman. Devotees carry the kavadi as penance and thanksgiving, especially those who vowed it in return for a prayer answered.

What is a kavadi?

A decorated, often arched frame carried on the shoulders to a Murugan temple as an offering; some kavadis involve milk pots, and some devotees bear vel skewers or hooks pierced through the skin as a vow.

Where is Thaipusam celebrated?

At the Murugan temples of Tamil Nadu such as Palani and Tiruchendur, and most spectacularly at Batu Caves in Malaysia and in Singapore among the Tamil diaspora.

Which god is worshipped on Thaipusam?

Murugan (Kartikeya or Subrahmanya), the son of Shiva and Parvati and the god of war and victory.
