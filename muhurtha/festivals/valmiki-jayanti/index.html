When it falls

Full moon of Ashwina. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

Significance

Valmiki Jayanti, Pragat Diwas, honours Maharishi Valmiki, the sage who composed the Ramayana, the first epic poem (adi kavya). Once a hunter named Ratnakara, he was transformed by a sage's teaching; moved to grief and verse by the killing of a bird, he spoke the first shloka and went on to tell the story of Rama.

The story

Valmiki began as a hunter named Ratnakara and became a great ascetic after a sage's teaching. Moved by the grief of a bird whose mate had been shot, he spoke his first shloka and went on to compose the Ramayana.

How it is kept

There are processions, recitations of the Ramayana and community meals. The day is especially marked by the Valmiki community.

Regional and community variations

It is kept across India on the full moon of Ashwina, especially by the Valmiki community, with processions (shobha yatras), Ramayana recitation and community meals.

Foods and prasad

Community meals (langar) and prasad are shared at the processions and temples.

What to do, what to avoid

Hear or recite the Ramayana, join the shobha yatra, and reflect on Valmiki's turn from darkness to wisdom; the day celebrates the power of transformation and of the word.

Common questions

When is Valmiki Jayanti in 2026?

In 2026, Valmiki Jayanti falls on Monday, 26 October 2026 in Mumbai. Dates for other years are in the table above.

What is the significance of Valmiki Jayanti?

It honours Maharishi Valmiki, author of the Ramayana and the first poet (adi kavi), whose life shows that anyone can rise from darkness to wisdom.

What is the story of Valmiki's transformation?

Once the hunter Ratnakara, he was set on the path of penance by a sage; grief at seeing a hunter kill one of a pair of birds moved him to utter the first shloka, and he composed the Ramayana.

What is the Ramayana?

The epic of Rama composed by Valmiki, the adi kavya or first poem, which tells of Rama's life, exile, the rescue of Sita and the triumph of dharma.
