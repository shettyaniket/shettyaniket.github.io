When it falls

12th day of the waxing moon, in Bhadrapada. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

Significance

Vamana Jayanti marks the appearance of Vamana, the dwarf, the fifth of Vishnu's ten avatars. The righteous but proud king Bali had conquered the three worlds; Vamana, a small Brahmin, asked for three paces of land, then grew vast and covered the earth and sky in two strides, sending Bali to the netherworld with the third — yet blessing him for his honesty and generosity.

The story

King Bali, Prahlada's grandson, grew so powerful that the gods feared for their place. Vishnu came as Vamana, a dwarf Brahmin, and asked Bali for three paces of land. He measured the sky with one and the earth with another, and Bali offered his own head for the third, and was made ruler of the netherworld.

How it is kept

Devotees fast, worship an image of Vamana and offer curd rice. In Kerala the story is bound up with Onam, when Bali is believed to visit his people.

Fasting and parana

Devotees fast, worship an image of Vamana, usually at midday (his appearance is placed at noon), and offer curd rice before breaking the fast.

Regional and community variations

It is kept across the Vaishnava world; in Kerala the story is woven into Onam, when Bali (Mahabali) is believed to return to visit his people once a year.

Foods and prasad

Curd rice and sattvic offerings are made to Vamana; in Kerala the Onam connection brings the great sadya feast in the same season.

What to do, what to avoid

Keep the fast and worship Vamana, remembering the lesson of Bali — that pride is humbled but true generosity is honoured even in defeat.

Common questions

When is Vamana Jayanti in 2027?

In 2027, Vamana Jayanti falls on Sunday, 12 September 2027 in Mumbai. Dates for other years are in the table above.

What is the significance of Vamana Jayanti?

It marks the appearance of Vamana, the dwarf, Vishnu's fifth avatar, who humbled the proud king Bali in three strides yet honoured his generosity by making him ruler of the netherworld.

Which avatar of Vishnu is Vamana?

The fifth of the ten avatars (Dashavatara) — the dwarf Brahmin who reclaimed the three worlds from Bali in three paces.

How is Vamana Jayanti connected to Onam?

The king Bali of the Vamana story is the Mahabali of Kerala's Onam; Onam celebrates his annual return to visit his people, by the boon Vishnu granted him.

When is Vamana worshipped on his Jayanti?

At midday, the hour traditionally given for his appearance, after a day's fast.
