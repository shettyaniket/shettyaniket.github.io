Vivah Panchami

The wedding of Rama and Sita, kept in Janakpur and Ayodhya.

Dates are for Mumbai. A tithi that changes close to sunrise or sunset can fall a day earlier or later in another city, so for your own town open the festival in the app.

When it falls

5th day of the waxing moon, in Margashirsha. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

At Sita's swayamvara in Janakpur, Rama strung Shiva's great bow, and they were married on this day. In some regions people avoid holding weddings on it because of the hardships Sita later faced.

How it is kept

At Janakpur's Janaki Mandir the wedding of the idols is staged with a procession, and devotees read the Ramcharitmanas. Ayodhya also celebrates.
