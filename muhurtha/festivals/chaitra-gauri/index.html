When it falls

3rd day of the waxing moon, in Chaitra. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

From the third day of Chaitra's bright half until Akshaya Tritiya, Parvati as Gauri is believed to visit her mother's home. Households install her image on a decorated swing and welcome women as guests.

How it is kept

Each day women are invited for haldi-kunku, given soaked chana and mango panha, and sing songs, while the goddess's swing is decked with flowers.
