When it falls

7th day of the waxing moon, in Magha. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

Shri Advaita Acharya appeared in Bengal in 1434, on the seventh day of the bright fortnight of Magha. A great scholar and devotee, he is said to have prayed for Krishna to appear on earth, and was among the first associates of Chaitanya Mahaprabhu.

How it is kept

Devotees fast until noon, chant, and read about his life.
