When it falls

8th day of the waning moon, in Kartika. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

Significance

Kalabhairav Jayanti marks the appearance of Bhairava, the fierce form of Shiva who arose to humble Brahma's pride, severing his fifth head. As Kala Bhairava he is the lord of time and the kotwal (guardian) of Kashi, before whom even pilgrims must present themselves; his worship removes fear, obstacles and the effects of Saturn and the planets.

The story

Bhairava is the fierce form of Shiva that appeared to punish Brahma's pride by cutting off Brahma's fifth head. He guards time and is the protector of Kashi.

How it is kept

Devotees fast and worship Kalabhairava at night with black sesame and mustard-oil lamps, and feed dogs, his companion. The Kal Bhairav temple in Kashi sees large crowds.

Fasting and parana

Devotees fast and worship Kala Bhairava at night with black sesame, mustard-oil lamps and offerings, and feed dogs, his vahana and companion; the fast is broken after the night worship.

Regional and community variations

It is kept across India, grandest at the Kal Bhairav temple in Kashi (Varanasi), where he is the guardian of the city, and at Bhairava shrines in Ujjain and elsewhere.

Foods and prasad

Offerings vary by tradition; black sesame, urad, and in some shrines liquor are offered to Bhairava, and dogs are fed as his sacred animal.

What to do, what to avoid

Worship Bhairava at night, light mustard-oil lamps, feed dogs, and seek his protection from fear and obstacles; he is the stern but protective guardian deity.

Common questions

When is Kalabhairav Jayanti in 2026?

In 2026, Kalabhairav Jayanti falls on Tuesday, 1 December 2026 in Mumbai. Dates for other years are in the table above.

What is the significance of Kalabhairav Jayanti?

It marks the appearance of Kala Bhairava, the fierce form of Shiva and lord of time, guardian of Kashi; his worship is believed to remove fear, obstacles and planetary troubles.

Why are dogs fed on Kalabhairav Jayanti?

The dog is Kala Bhairava's vahana and companion, so feeding dogs is part of his worship and considered especially pleasing to him.

How is Kala Bhairava worshipped?

At night, with black sesame, urad, mustard-oil lamps and offerings, and the feeding of dogs; the Kal Bhairav temple in Kashi is the chief centre.
