When it falls

The new moon while the Sun is in Cancer. Like every lunar festival it lands on a different Gregorian date each year. Muhurtha works the date out from the positions of the Sun and Moon, using the rule for this festival; see how every festival is dated.

The story

Aati is the Tulu monsoon month, and its new moon is the day for drinking the bitter bark decoction of the paale tree, called paleda kashaya. Tradition says it protects against the illnesses of the rains.

How it is kept

Before dawn, families collect the bark of the paale tree, grind it with herbs and garlic and drink the kashaya. They also eat methi kanji and special Aati dishes.
