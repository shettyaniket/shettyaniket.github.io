Muhurtha — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

The short version

Muhurtha collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. The app makes no network calls at all for the Panchang. Every calculation runs on your device, and nothing you enter leaves it unless you explicitly share it.

There is no server

Muhurtha carries its own astronomical engine and its own festival rules. It does not download a calendar, check for dates, or call an API to answer a question. There is no backend for me to run, which means there is nothing for me to store about you and nothing to be breached.

Your data stays on your device

Saved locations, the birth details you enter under You & family, which festival traditions you follow, your reminder preferences and your chosen month system are stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. They are never sent anywhere, and I cannot access them. Deleting the app removes them.

Birth details

The dates, times, places and names you enter for yourself or your family are treated like any other setting: stored locally, never transmitted, never backed up to a service of mine. They exist so the app can work out a nakshatra and tell you when a tithi comes round. That calculation happens on the device.

Location

Sunrise, sunset and every window in the Panchang depend on where you are, so the app asks for your location while in use. The coordinates are used on the device to compute the day and are not sent anywhere. You can refuse the permission and pick a city by hand instead — the app is fully usable that way, and ships with a bundled list of cities so search works offline.

City search

Searching the bundled city list is entirely offline. If you search for somewhere not in that list and you have a network connection, the app asks Apple's own geocoder on your device to resolve the name. That request goes to Apple, not to me, under Apple's privacy policy. Nothing else about you accompanies it.

Notifications and Live Activities

Reminders for Rahu Kaal, fasts, festivals, Brahma Muhurat and the sunrise Panchang are scheduled locally by your device using Apple's UserNotifications framework. There are no push notifications, so there is no device token and no server sending you anything. The optional Lock Screen countdown uses ActivityKit and runs entirely on the device.

Apple Calendar

If you choose to add festivals and fasts to Apple Calendar, the app asks for write-only calendar access and creates the events in a calendar you pick. It cannot read your existing events, and the permission iOS grants it does not allow that.

Widgets

The Home Screen widgets read the same locally computed Panchang through a private App Group shared with the app. That data never leaves your device.

Sharing

Anything you share from the app is handed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there — Messages, WhatsApp, Notes — is entirely your choice. The app never sends it anywhere itself.

Purchases

There are none. Muhurtha is free, with no in-app purchases, no subscription and no advertising.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date. Since the app has no server, any change would come with an app update you choose to install.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about the app: Support.
