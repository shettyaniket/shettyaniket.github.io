Pace & Sip

Pace & Sip is the friend who quietly keeps count and wants you to enjoy the night — and get home fine. Log a drink in a tap and watch the screen ease from green toward red as you approach your limit. From one drink before it, your phone and watch start nudging you to slow down. A Water button counts down to your next sip of water, a ride home is always one tap away, and when you're done you close the tab for the night. No account, no cloud, no subscription. Everything lives on your phone.

A buddy for the night, not a lecture.

Built for people who want to drink on their own terms and still keep an eye on the pace.

🟢 A gauge that fills as you drink

The whole screen eases from green toward red with every drink, so you can see how the night is going at a glance — and it reaches full red at your limit, not before.

🫶 A buddy, not a scold

Rotating, friendly lines instead of warnings. It nudges you to slow down from one drink before your cap — by haptic and notification on your phone and your wrist — and celebrates when you close the tab on time.

💧 Water, on a visible clock

The Water button counts down to your next reminder (you set the gap) and fills from blue to red as it nears zero. Log a glass and the clock resets.

🌍 Drinks measured your way

Shots, pints, pegs, tots — presets follow your region (oz or ml), and you can add your own. Up to 100, all editable.

🚕 A ride home, always one tap away

Detects which of Uber, Lyft, Ola and Bolt are installed and opens your preferred one. Text a buddy for a pickup. Never hidden behind a limit.

⌚ Phone, widget, Dynamic Island, Watch

Log a drink or water from the Home Screen, Lock Screen, Dynamic Island or Apple Watch without opening the app.

🌙 Closes the tab for you

Tap Close Tab for the Night to end the session with a little celebration. Forget? After two hours of nothing, it checks in — and closes the tab so it isn't left open all night.

🔒 Fully offline, fully private

No account, no cloud, no ads, no analytics. Everything stays on your phone.

A note on what it is — and isn't

Pace & Sip counts the drinks you log and nudges you to pace yourself. It is not a breathalyser or a medical device, can't tell you whether you're safe to drive, and doesn't replace your own judgment. If you've been drinking, get a ride.
