LoanYield IQ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

The short version

LoanYield IQ collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. Every calculation runs on your device and nothing you enter leaves it unless you explicitly share it.

Your data stays on your device

Property deals, loans, prepayment schedules and settings are stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework on your iPhone or iPad. This data is not synced to any server, not shared with any third party, and not accessible to me. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app renders an image of the verdict on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there — WhatsApp, Messages, Photos — is entirely your choice. The app never sends it anywhere itself.

Camera and document scanning

The cost-sheet scanner uses Apple's VisionKit to recognise text on-device. Camera frames are processed locally and are never stored or transmitted. You can deny camera access and the feature simply offers a paste box instead.

AI memo

By default the underwriting memo is generated on-device with Apple Intelligence, or by the app's own rules engine when that isn't available. Nothing is sent to any server.

Optional API keys

If you choose to add your own API key for a cloud provider (Anthropic, Google or OpenAI) under Settings, that key is stored in your device's Keychain — not in the app's database. It is used only to send the memo request directly from your device to the provider you chose, and only when you tap Generate. LoanYield IQ never sees, stores or transmits the key or the request anywhere else. That provider's own privacy policy applies to what you send it.

Siri

The prepayment shortcut runs entirely on-device through Apple's App Intents. Your loan details are not sent to Apple by the app.

Not financial advice

LoanYield IQ is a modelling tool with visible assumptions. It does not provide financial, tax or legal advice. Consult a qualified professional before making a property or loan decision.

Children's privacy

LoanYield IQ does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date above.
