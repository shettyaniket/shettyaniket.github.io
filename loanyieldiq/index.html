LoanYield IQ

Should I buy this flat? LoanYield IQ is a free, offline app that answers that question honestly for Indian home buyers. Type in the agreement value, the rent it would fetch, and your loan — and it works out the all-in cost with stamp duty, registration and GST for your state, the cash you'll burn on pre-EMI before an under-construction flat is handed over, the Section 24 tax shield on your rent and interest, your true 10-year return with the loan in the picture, and what the very same cheques would have earned in a Nifty SIP or by prepaying debt. Then it gives you a one-line verdict — Buy, Marginal, or Rent + SIP instead — under three price-growth scenarios, so you can see exactly what the decision hinges on. Every number has an ⓘ that explains it in plain English. And for the loan you already have: import the bank's statement or amortization schedule, plan prepayments and EMI step-ups as scenarios, and ask a built-in advisor which loan to close first or what it takes to be debt-free in five years. No account, no ads, nothing leaves your phone.

What it is — and what it isn't.

It is

  • A second opinion that shows its working — tap ⓘ on any number for the plain-English explanation.
  • Built for the under-construction purchase, which is where Indian buyers lose the most money quietly — and for the loan you already carry.
  • Engine-first: every number the advisor chat or the AI memo says is computed, never generated.
  • Free, with no upsell. There is no Pro tier and there won't be one.
  • Private by construction: SwiftData on your device, Keychain for any optional API key, no network calls unless you add one.

It isn't

  • Financial advice. It's a model with visible assumptions; your CA and your own judgement come first.
  • A rate feed. Stamp duty and GST schedules are built in with the dates shown; states change them and so will the app.
  • Complete for every state yet — Maharashtra, Karnataka and Delhi are modelled in detail; others use a generic 6% until added.
  • A substitute for reading the agreement. Selling costs, brokerage and society transfer fees are not modelled.

The spreadsheet I kept rebuilding.

Every time someone I know was about to book a flat, the same spreadsheet came out: stamp duty, GST, what the rent would cover, what the pre-EMI would cost during construction, and the awkward column at the end — what the same money would have done in a plain index fund. Every builder's cost sheet skipped half of it. Every bank calculator skipped all of it.

LoanYield IQ is that spreadsheet, made honest and made shareable. It won't tell you what you want to hear. It will tell you what the decision actually hinges on — usually price growth — and let you see the answer under a cautious view as well as a hopeful one.

No account. No subscription. Everything on your phone, yours completely.
