Forehead Flip — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 July 2026

The short version

Forehead Flip does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, and no third-party tracking or analytics. Nothing about you or your family is sent to me or to anyone else.

Word decks & AI themes

When you generate a word deck from a theme you type, that theme text is sent to an AI service solely to create the list of words and return it to your device. It is used only to generate your deck, is not linked to your identity, and no personal information is included or stored. The words you play with stay on your device.

Third-party services

Forehead Flip does not integrate any advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Children's privacy

Forehead Flip is family-friendly and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
