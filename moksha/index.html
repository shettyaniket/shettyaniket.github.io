How to Play Mokṣa Paṭam (YesVedanta Ver 3.0 Rules)

18th-Century 8×9 Gyandev Board · Game of Self-Inquiry

1. Beginning the Game: Why Start at #68 ➔ #1 ➔ #6?

  • Place your token on Square #68 (Vaikuṇṭha / Pure Consciousness): Before taking birth, your true nature is already whole, non-dual Awareness.
  • Roll a 6 to Enter (Solo Play): When you roll a 6, move your token first to #1 Janma (Birth)—pause to contemplate what it means to be born—and then immediately place your token on #6 Moha (Delusion / Past Conditioning), because every human birth arrives carrying past impressions (saṃskāras).
  • 2–3 Players Note: As Acharya Andre notes in the comments, when playing with 2 or 3 people you can toggle the top bar to allow starting on any dice roll so players don't wait too long.

2. Virtues (10 Ladders ↑)

Landing on the base of a ladder shows how discrimination, purity (#10 Śuddhi), compassion (#17 Dayā), dharma (#22), right knowledge (#45 Suvidyā), and devotion (#54 Bhakti) elevate your state of mind.

3. Pitfalls (10 Snakes ↓)

No matter how high you climb—even to #55 Ahaṃkāra (Spiritual Ego) or #63 Tamas—you are never immune to a slip until realization. Snakes remind the seeker to stay humble and vigilant.

4. Reaching #72 & Finishing at #68

  • If You Reach or Pass #72 (Tamoguṇa): In accordance with the board rules ("If reach 72, continue counting from 51"), overshooting #68 into the upper Guna squares (69–72) and reaching #72 wraps your remaining steps directly into #51 Pṛthvī (Earthly Rebirth).
  • Attaining Mokṣa (#68): You complete the game when you land squarely on #68 Vaikuṇṭha-Loka (either by exact dice roll or via the Ladder of Devotion from #54 Bhakti-Loka ➔ #68).
